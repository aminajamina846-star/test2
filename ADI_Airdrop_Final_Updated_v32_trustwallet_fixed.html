ADI Network ($ADI) - Official Technical White Paper

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION

ADI Network ($ADI) is a decentralized micro-mining protocol architected on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). The platform eliminates high hardware and energy barriers traditional mining requires, replacing them with a lightweight Tap-to-Mine ecosystem integrated into Web3 platforms like Telegram Mini Apps.

By leveraging high transaction speeds and minimal gas fees on the BNB Smart Chain, ADI Network offers global users direct access to decentralized token generation, instant referral rewards, and on-chain verified allocations prior to mainnet listing.

Testnet Smart Contract Address:
0x93003783e72CC160990FA99A029b40cB74f63B9E

2. TOKENOMICS ARCHITECTURE

Total Supply: 10,000,000 $ADI (Hard Capped)
Network: BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)
Distribution Breakdown:
• Community Airdrop: 40% (4,000,000 $ADI)
Distributed post-listing to verified miners & top referral partners.
• Exchange Liquidity: 30% (3,000,000 $ADI)
Paired with collected BNB/USDT revenues and locked into PancakeSwap LP.
• Core Team & Development: 20% (2,000,000 $ADI)
Infrastructure overhead, smart contract audits, and core upgrades.
• Marketing & Growth: 10% (1,000,000 $ADI)
Promotional mini-giveaways, KOL partnerships, and exchange listing reserves.

3. OFFICIAL PROJECT ROADMAP

Phase 1: Testnet Deployment & Scaling (Active)
  • Smart contract deployed and verified on BNB Smart Chain Testnet.
  • Tap-to-Mine engine and viral referral mechanism live across community channels.
Phase 2: Revenue Collection & Liquidity Build-Up (Days 1–40)
  • 100% of community ad revenues, bot monetization, and channel sponsorships are continuously funnelled into liquidity pool reserves for PancakeSwap injection.
Phase 3: Mainnet Launch, DEX Listing & Airdrop Claim (Nov 20, 2026)
  • BEP-20 Mainnet contract release.
  • Direct LP pairing on PancakeSwap enabling instant swaps ($ADI to BNB/USDT).
  • Immediate opening of the 4 Million $ADI Airdrop claim portal for miners.
